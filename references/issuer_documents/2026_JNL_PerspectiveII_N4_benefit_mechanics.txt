prospectus utilizes Rate Sheet Prospectus Supplements to describe the current annual charges,

============================================================================================
BONUS
============================================================================================

"A bonus equal to a percentage of the Bonus Base (defined below) will be applied to the GWB at
the end of each Contract Year within the Bonus Period (also defined below) if no withdrawals
are taken during that Contract Year."

On the Bonus Base:

"When this GMWB is added to the Contract, the Bonus Base equals the GWB."

"With a withdrawal, if that withdrawal, and all prior withdrawals in the current Contract Year,
exceeds the Earnings-Sensitive Adjustments during that Contract Year plus the greater of the
GAWA or the RMD, as applicable, then the Bonus Base is set to the lesser of the GWB after, and
the Bonus Base before, the withdrawal. Otherwise, there is no adjustment to the Bonus Base with
withdrawals."

"A withdrawal in a Contract Year during the Bonus Period precludes a bonus for that Contract
Year."

"With any step-up (if the GWB increases upon step-up), the Bonus Base is set to the greater of
the GWB after, and the Bonus Base before, the step-up."

"The GWB is recalculated, increasing by a percentage of the Bonus Base." and "Applying the
bonus to the GWB does not affect the Bonus Base or GWB adjustment."

On the Bonus Period:

"The Bonus Period begins on the effective date of this GMWB endorsement. In addition, the Bonus
Period will re-start at the time the Bonus Base increases due to a step-up so long as the
step-up occurs on or before the Contract Anniversary immediately following the Designated
Life's 80th birthday."

"The Bonus Period ends on the earlier of: The tenth Contract Anniversary following (1) the
effective date of the endorsement or (2) the most recent increase to the Bonus Base due to a
step-up, if later; or The date the Contract Value is zero."

What this settles for the model. The bonus is simple on the Bonus Base, not compound on the
GWB, because applying it does not feed the base. The only thing that lifts the base is a
step-up that itself raises the GWB, or a premium. An implementation that treats any rise in the
GWB as a step-up turns a 6% simple bonus into a 6% compound roll-up: 179 against 160 on a base
of 100 over a ten-year deferral. The clock restarting on step-ups also means a contract in a
rising market can collect a bonus for far longer than ten years, which is the path where the
guarantee is worth least, so the feature costs the insurer less than its headline suggests.

============================================================================================
GUARANTEED WITHDRAWAL BALANCE ADJUSTMENT
============================================================================================

"If no withdrawals are taken from the Contract on or prior to the GWB Adjustment Date (as
defined below), then you will receive a GWB adjustment."

"The GWB Adjustment Date is the later of: The Contract Anniversary on or immediately following
the Designated Life's 70th birthday, Or The 12th Contract Anniversary following the effective
date of this endorsement."

"On the effective date of this endorsement, the GWB adjustment is equal to a percentage of the
GWB, subject to a maximum of $10 million."

"If no withdrawals are taken on or prior to the GWB Adjustment Date, the GWB will be re-set on
that date to equal the greater of the current GWB or the GWB adjustment. No adjustments are
made to the Bonus Base or the GMWB. Once the GWB is re-set, this GWB adjustment provision
terminates. In addition, if a withdrawal is taken on or before the GWB Adjustment Date, this
GWB adjustment provision terminates without value."

What this settles. The adjustment is a floor on the benefit base equal to 105% of the benefit
base at issue on the Core and Value options and 200% on Plus, and it is paid for by never
taking a withdrawal - not even a required minimum distribution, which the prospectus flags
separately for tax-qualified contracts. A contract that starts income on the anniversary at
age 70 loses it, because that withdrawal falls on the adjustment date. The provision is
therefore dormant for most of the base-case grid and live only for cohorts that defer past it,
which is one reason the deferral-phase cohorts had to be modelled separately.

============================================================================================
BASIC DEATH BENEFIT
============================================================================================

"The death benefit equals the greater of: your Contract Value on the date we receive all
required documentation from your Beneficiary; or the total Premiums you have paid since your
Contract was issued reduced for prior withdrawals (including any applicable charges and
adjustments) in the same proportion that the Contract Value was reduced on the date of the
withdrawal."

Proportional, not dollar for dollar. A withdrawal taken while the contract is down cuts the
death benefit by more than the cash withdrawn.

============================================================================================
ADD-ON DEATH BENEFITS
============================================================================================

Availability: "The Roll-up GMDB, Highest Quarterly Anniversary Value GMDB, and Combination
Roll-up and Highest Quarterly Anniversary Value GMDB are available if you are 79 years of age
or younger on the Contract's Issue Date."

Highest quarterly anniversary value, from Appendix E:

  "The death benefit payable under the Contract will be equal to the larger of your current
  Contract Value or GMDB Benefit Base."
  Set at election to the contract value. Re-determined on each Contract Quarterly Anniversary
  to "the larger of your Contract Value or GMDB Benefit Base".
  Withdrawals: base cut by "a reduction of 10%, the same proportion that the Contract Value is
  reduced for the withdrawal" - worked through at $15,000 out of $150,000 taking a base from
  $160,000 to $144,000.
  "The Contract Quarterly Anniversaries in the above scenarios are assumed to occur prior to
  the oldest Covered Life's 81st birthday."

Roll-up, from Appendix E:

  Base set at election to the initial premium, and compounded annually. "Your GMDB Benefit Base
  becomes $105,000 ($100,000 * (1 + 5%) = $105,000) on the first Contract Anniversary...
  becomes $110,250 at the second Contract Anniversary". Compound, unlike the GMWB bonus.
  "The compounding at an annual interest rate only occurs until the Contract Anniversary
  immediately preceding the oldest Covered Life's 81st birthday."
  Withdrawals: "reduced first dollar-for-dollar for up to 5% of your GMDB Benefit Base... The
  GMDB Benefit Base is then reduced by the percentage reduction in the Contract Value
  attributable to the withdrawals in excess of the 5%". Worked example: a $24,000 withdrawal
  against a $150,000 contract value and a $200,000 base gives
  ($200,000 - $10,000) * (1 - ($24,000 - $10,000)/($150,000 - $10,000)) = $171,000.
  A withdrawal inside the 5% is dollar for dollar only: $5,000 takes $200,000 to $195,000.

  Note on rates. The Appendix E examples use 5% up to issue age 69 and 4% from 70, which were
  the rates when those examples were written. The current rate sheet gives 6% and 5%, and those
  are what the model uses. Both tests in tests/test_liability.py that reproduce the appendix
  arithmetic use the appendix's own numbers, so they check the mechanics rather than the rates.

Combination: "your GMDB Benefit Base is the greater of your Roll-Up Component and Highest
Quarterly Anniversary Value" component.

============================================================================================
WHAT IS NOT MODELLED, AND WHY
============================================================================================

The Earnings-Sensitive Adjustment on the Flex Net GMWB, which lets the owner draw more than the
GAWA in a year when the contract value has grown. It interacts with the Bonus Base rule quoted
above and there is no free data on how often owners use it.

Subsequent premium payments. Every cohort is single premium, so the recalculation rules for the
GWB, the Bonus Base and the GMDB benefit base on later premiums are not exercised.

The quarterly step-up on the Plus option and the quarterly ratchet on the highest-anniversary
death benefit, both modelled annually. An annual ratchet is a floor on a quarterly one, so both
are understated, and by how much depends on how far the contract value round-trips inside a
year.
