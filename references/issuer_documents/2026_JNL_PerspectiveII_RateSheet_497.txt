Why the rate sheet and not the prospectus. The Perspective II statutory prospectus
(Form N-4, 485BPOS filed 21 April 2026, accession 0000927730-26-000195) gives the mechanics
but not the numbers: it states that "This prospectus utilizes Rate Sheet Prospectus
Supplements to describe the current annual charges, Bonus percentages, Guaranteed Withdrawal
Balance Adjustment percentages, and Guaranteed Annual Withdrawal Amount percentages for
certain add-on living and death benefits." The prospectus quotes only a maximum charge of
3.00% of the benefit base, which is a cap on what Jackson may charge and not what it does
charge. Modelling the maximum would overstate fee income by more than a factor of two. The
rates below are the current ones.

"The charges and rates below apply for applications or benefit election forms signed on or
after April 27, 2026."
