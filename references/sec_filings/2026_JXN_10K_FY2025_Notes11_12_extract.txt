Method        From EDGAR's own rendering of the filing's financial statements, one table per
              URL, rather than copied out of the main document. Each table below carries the
              report file it came from. The FilingSummary that lists them is at
              https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1822993/000182299326000022/FilingSummary.xml
              and every R-file sits beside it under the same accession folder.
Used by       data/raw/jackson_book_statistics.csv, every column of it, which in turn sets the
              equity weight of the sub-account, the cash surrender value share in
              vahedge/capital/statutory.py, and the scaling denominator for every disclosed
              sensitivity.

Why this exists separately from the Item 7A extract: the sensitivity tables this project
validates against are Item 7A, and they were preserved from the start. Notes 11 and 12 supply
the book the sensitivities are scaled by, and were not. An audit found 25 of the 46 figures in
jackson_book_statistics.csv had no stored source, among them the separate account value and the
fund split. Worse, the one cash surrender value the repository did store - 6,330 for variable
annuities, in the notes extract beside this one - is the general-account figure from Note 10,
and a reader checking the 98.0% surrender share against it would be out by a factor of
thirty-seven. The number that share is built on is the last row of the Note 11 rollforward
below.

Every figure in jackson_book_statistics.csv for 31 December 2024 and 31 December 2025 was
checked against these tables when they were retrieved, and they all agree.

What is still not preserved
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The 2022 and 2023 rows of jackson_book_statistics.csv come from the FY2023 10-K, accession
0001822993-24-000009, whose equivalent tables are not stored here. Three of those figures are
covered anyway by the comparative columns above - the 2023 net MRB of -2,000 in R128 and the
2023 separate account balance of 219,381 as R125's opening balance - and the rest are not. To
close that, the same five tables from the FY2023 filing's own FilingSummary would need
retrieving; the report numbers differ by filing, so they have to be read off that summary rather
than assumed from this one.
